# Goals

Performance targets with milestone ladders. Goals are proposed by Edu,
vetted for realism by the coach, broken into milestones, and tracked
against the log. Max 2-3 active at once — a wall of goals is a wall of
guilt.

## Phase 0 (now): get the ball rolling

The only goal is the streak: consistent sessions, log filling up, system
refined through use. No performance targets until the base is back.

**Exit criteria** (coach proposes goal-setting when these hit):
- ~4-6 weeks of consistent training logged
- Baseline tests done: working bench/row weights, max strict pull-ups,
  a comfortable belt squat working weight
